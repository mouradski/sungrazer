Sungrazer

One-finger arcade game — support

Français

Your planet orbits a blazing sun. Hold to dive toward it, release to swing away. Graze the sun to multiply your stars, but get too close and you burn.

How to play

  • Hold anywhere to dive toward the sun, release to drift away.
  • Don't touch the sun. Past the heat ring it slows you down, then burns you.
  • Graze it and get out alive: your stars are worth ×2, even ×3, for 3 seconds.
  • Chain stars to build your combo. Golden stars, right next to the sun, are worth ×5 (×8 around Sirius B).
  • Every star draws part of a constellation. Finish it for a bonus.
  • A red dot on the edge of the screen warns of an incoming asteroid.
  • About every 30 seconds an event hits: solar storm, star shower, asteroid swarm or eclipse.
  • Pick up the bonuses that appear on your orbit: the shield first, then magnet, slow motion, double points and shockwave as you level up.
  • Complete missions and earn experience every run to level up. Levels unlock new star systems with their own rules, and new planets.
  • A new daily challenge every day: beat it several days in a row to grow your streak.

Frequently asked

My score doesn't show on the leaderboard

The world leaderboards use Game Center. Open Settings › Game Center and sign in: your records are sent right away, then each run's score at its end. Each star system has its own leaderboard, and daily-challenge scores are not ranked.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Your level, scores, medals and streak are kept as long as the app is installed. Deleting the app deletes them: there is no iCloud sync. Only your record around the Sun comes back from Game Center if you sign in again.

How do I switch between English and French?

Sungrazer follows your iPhone's preferred languages: it takes the first one it speaks, French or English, and English otherwise. If your iPhone lists several preferred languages, you can also pick one just for the game in Settings › Apps › Sungrazer › Language.

Can I turn off the music, sounds or vibrations?

Yes: tap the gear on the main screen. You can also reduce animations there, and replay the in-game tips.

Does it work on iPad?

Sungrazer is made for iPhone (iOS 18 or later), in portrait. On iPad it runs as an iPhone app.
