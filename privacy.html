Privacy Policy

Sungrazer — last updated 25 November 2026

Français

Sungrazer itself collects nothing: no account, no analytics and no server of its own. Since version 1.1 it shows ads from Google, which use information from your device; Game Center, which is optional, handles the rankings and the achievements. This page says what each of them receives.

What the game collects

Nothing by itself. Sungrazer has no server: it does not collect, store, sell or send us any personal data. No sign-up, no user identifier of its own, no crash or usage reports of its own. What Google receives through the ads is described below. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, under Apple's own policy.

What stays on your device

Your scores, level, missions, medals, achievements, daily-challenge streak, chosen planet, the tips you have seen and your settings are saved on your device, in the app's own storage. If you use Game Center, the app also keeps there the Game Center player ID your records and achievements belong to. Deleting the app deletes all of it. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are also sent to it (see below), and your record around the Sun comes back from it when you sign in again.

The daily challenge reminder is off unless you turn it on. It is a notification that iOS prepares on your device: nothing is sent to us or to anyone, and you can turn it off in the game's options or in Settings › Notifications.

Ads

A full-screen ad is shown at the end of a run, after five runs and at most once every 15 minutes, never during play. It is provided by Google AdMob, and the game only asks for ads that Google rates as suitable for all audiences. To choose and measure these ads, and to fight fraud, Google's software built into the game collects and sends to Google: the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate its approximate location), your interactions with the app and the ads (launches, taps, ads seen), diagnostic and performance information, and the identifier iOS gives the app's developer on this device. Sungrazer does not ask for permission to track you, so the iPhone's advertising identifier is never available to Google. This data is encrypted in transit. Google handles it under its privacy policy; this Google page explains how it uses information from apps that use its services. We receive none of this data.

Your choice

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for your consent at first launch, before any ad. You can change it at any time in the game: Options, then "Ad privacy choices".

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the score of each run is sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards (its star system's, or the daily challenge's for a daily run), along with your record in each star system, and the achievements you earn are sent to Game Center's achievements. The leaderboard screen shows the Game Center nicknames of ranked players. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we receive no data from it. Game Center is optional: you can play without signing in, and manage it in Settings › Game Center.

Third parties

The only third-party software in Sungrazer is Google's, for the ads: Google Mobile Ads and its consent message (User Messaging Platform). No other advertising, analytics or tracking service.

Children

Sungrazer is suitable for all ages (rated 4+ on the App Store), and its ads are limited to those Google rates as suitable for all audiences. It is not directed at children and does not seek to collect data from them.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right to access, correct and erase your personal data. We hold none. For the data Google processes for advertising, you can exercise your rights with Google, and you can withdraw your consent to advertising in the game, as described above. If you write to the address below, your message is kept only for as long as it takes to answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
