Privacy Policy

Sungrazer — last updated 9 November 2026

Français

Sungrazer collects nothing. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no networking code of its own. Your progress stays on your device.

What is collected

Nothing. Sungrazer does not collect, store on a server, transmit or sell any personal data. There is no sign-up, no user identifier of our own, no advertising identifier, and no crash or usage reporting of its own. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports and usage statistics, under Apple's own policy.

What stays on your device

Your scores, level, missions, medals, daily-challenge streak, chosen planet, the tips you have seen and your settings are saved on your device, in the app's own storage. If you use Game Center, the app also keeps there the Game Center player ID your records belong to. Deleting the app deletes all of it. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are also sent to it (see below), and your record around the Sun comes back from it when you sign in again.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the score of each run (daily challenges excepted) and your record in each star system are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, and the leaderboard screen shows the Game Center nicknames of ranked players. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we receive no data from it. Game Center is optional: you can play without signing in, and manage it in Settings › Game Center.

Third parties

Sungrazer contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks and no analytics services. No data is shared with anyone, because none is gathered.

Children

Sungrazer is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification and erasure over your personal data. We hold none, so there is nothing to access, correct or delete. If you contact the address below, your message is kept only as long as needed to answer you.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date. Should a future version of Sungrazer ever collect anything, it will be stated here plainly, and before the fact.
