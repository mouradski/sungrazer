Privacy Policy

Sungrazer for Android — updated 19 November 2026

Français

On Android, Sungrazer itself collects nothing: no account of its own, no analytics, no server of its own. It uses two Google services: ads, which use information from your device, and Google Play Games, which is optional, for the world rankings and the achievements. This page says what each of them receives. The iPhone version has its own policy.

What the game collects

Nothing by itself. Sungrazer has no server: it does not collect, store, sell or send us any personal data. No sign-up, no user identifier of its own, no crash or usage reports of its own. What Google receives through the ads and through Google Play Games is described below.

What stays on your device

Your scores, your best runs, your level, missions, medals, achievements, daily challenge streak, planet, the tips you have seen and your settings are stored on your device, in the app's storage. None of it is sent or backed up anywhere else, except your scores and achievements when you are signed in to Google Play Games (see below). Deleting the app erases what is on your device.

Ads

A full-screen ad is shown after one run in five. It is provided by Google AdMob. To choose and measure these ads, and to fight fraud, Google's software built into the game collects and sends to Google: the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate its approximate location), your interactions with the app (launches, taps, video views), diagnostic information about performance, and device identifiers (the Android advertising ID and the app set ID). This data is encrypted in transit. Google handles it under its privacy policy; this Google page explains how it uses information from apps that use its services. We receive none of this data.

Your choice

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for your consent at first launch, before any ad. You can change it at any time in the game: Options, then "Ad privacy choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Privacy, then Ads).

Google Play Games

The world rankings and the online achievements go through Google Play Games, and only when you are signed in to it. If you have a Play Games profile, Play Games may sign you in when the game starts; otherwise the game offers a sign-in button. You can play without signing in: your scores and achievements then stay on your device.

When you are signed in, the game sends Google your score at the end of a run and the achievements you unlock, and Google gives the game your gamer name, which the game only displays. Your scores appear on the rankings under your gamer name; other players see them if your Play Games profile shares your game activity with everyone. Google's Play Games software also collects diagnostic and usage information to keep its service working. This data is encrypted in transit and handled by Google under its privacy policy. We receive none of it: the game has no server.

You can see and delete your Play Games data, Sungrazer's included, from your Play Games profile or your Google Account.

Notifications

The daily challenge reminder is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, it is a notification prepared on your device: nothing is sent.

Third parties

The only third-party software in Sungrazer for Android is Google's: for the ads (Google Mobile Ads and its consent message), and for the rankings and achievements (Google Play Games Services). No other advertising, analytics or tracking service.

Children

Sungrazer for Android is meant for adults, aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and does not seek to collect data from them.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right to access, correct and erase your personal data. We hold none. For the data Google processes for advertising and for Google Play Games, you can exercise your rights with Google; you can withdraw your consent to advertising in the game, and delete your Play Games data, as described above. If you write to the address below, your message is kept only for as long as it takes to answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
