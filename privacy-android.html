Privacy Policy

Sungrazer for Android — updated 18 November 2026

Français

On Android, Sungrazer itself collects nothing: no account, no analytics, no server of its own. It does show ads, provided by Google, which use information from your device. This page says which. The iPhone version has no ads: its policy is here.

What the game collects

Nothing. Sungrazer does not collect, store on any server, transmit or sell any personal data. No sign-up, no user identifier of its own, no crash or usage reports of its own.

What stays on your device

Your scores, your best runs, your level, missions, medals, achievements, daily challenge streak, planet, the tips you have seen and your settings are stored on your device, in the app's storage. None of it is sent or backed up anywhere else. Deleting the app erases everything.

Ads

A full-screen ad is shown after one run in five. It is provided by Google AdMob. To choose and measure these ads, and to fight fraud, Google's software built into the game collects and sends to Google: the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate its approximate location), your interactions with the app (launches, taps, video views), diagnostic information about performance, and device identifiers (the Android advertising ID and the app set ID). This data is encrypted in transit. Google handles it under its privacy policy; this Google page explains how it uses information from apps that use its services. We receive none of this data.

Your choice

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for your consent at first launch, before any ad. You can change it at any time in the game: Options, then "Ad privacy choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Privacy, then Ads).

Notifications

The daily challenge reminder is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, it is a notification prepared on your device: nothing is sent.

Third parties

The only third-party software in Sungrazer for Android is Google's, for the ads (Google Mobile Ads and its consent message). No other advertising, analytics or tracking service.

Children

Sungrazer for Android is meant for adults, aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children and does not seek to collect data from them.

Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right to access, correct and erase your personal data. We hold none. For the data Google processes for advertising, you can exercise your rights with Google, and withdraw your consent in the game as described above. If you write to the address below, your message is kept only for as long as it takes to answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
